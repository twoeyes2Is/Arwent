Who We Are
Who We Are

Around The World Entertainments

is an Afro-contemporary dance company and Black-owned enterprise founded in 2022 by Lundi Nkcubeko Mqhobo . Since its establishment, the company has consistently used dance and artistic talent to provide quality entertainment for audiences and education within the performing arts industry.

The company recruits talented and highly qualified performers to collaborate in building the enterprise. Around The World Entertainment creates a space where movement, theatre, music and education come together to celebrate African creativity and inspire global connection.

Vision

To become an esteemed company that people choose for a uniquely Pan-African experience.

Mission

  • To be an attractive entertainment destination that maintains a healthy balance of artistic creativity.
  • To empower artists and communities by providing platforms for talent development and meaningful storytelling.
  • To create and present high-quality theatrical works that address urgent social concerns.

Leadership

Ayabonga Mqhobo and Bongisa Mqhobo, Directors

Upcoming Shows

Discover performances that blend movement and theatre into vivid African narratives for local and international audiences.

It Her-ppened To Me

A powerful contemporary dance production exploring the harrowing experience of gender-based violence. The narrative follows Lerato, a young person whose loving relationship rapidly descends into a cycle of control and abuse by a partner.

iNkcubeko

A theatre-dance production highlighting a protagonist’s journey through cultural identity and heritage. Traditional African music and Afro-contemporary dance create a deeply personal exploration of identity, recognition and embracing who we are.
